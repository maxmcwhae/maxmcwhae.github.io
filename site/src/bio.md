# Max McWhae

I work on technical AI safety from Perth, Western Australia, and on making sure Perth has an AI safety community at all.

In 2026 I founded the Perth AI Safety Meetup, the city's first dedicated AI safety group, piloting sessions at UWA and Curtin with EA UWA. It is relaunching as a regular monthly series at UWA, cross-promoted with Perth’s machine learning community. Since 2025 I have also organised the Perth Astral Codex Ten Meetup.

I've studied technical AI safety since 2022, focusing on empirical alignment and agentic AI research workflows. I completed BlueDot Impact's Technical AI Safety course, I'm working through the ARENA curriculum, and I'm studying mathematics at UWA. I've been writing code since 2002, and everything I build ships open, including the negative results.

Before AI safety, I studied mining engineering at Curtin, tutored high school mathematics, and worked in warehousing, logistics and retail.

Open to collaboration, workshop facilitation, meetup speakers, and connecting with the Australian AI safety community.
